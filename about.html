About Kelly

Educator, curriculum designer, and lifelong learner based in Mount Shasta, California.

I never set out to become only one kind of educator. My path has woven together classroom teaching, alternative education program design, literacy tutoring, nature-based learning, and practical life skills education.

I hold a Master of Arts in Education from the University of California, Santa Cruz, along with early childhood education credentials. Over the years I have taught in traditional and alternative settings, designed full curricula for middle school critical thinking and American history, created specialized literacy programs, and developed grant-funded community education series.

Whether I’m helping a child discover the joy of reading through outdoor adventure, supporting a student who has struggled with math, or designing a complete learning program for a family or organization, my goal is the same: to create experiences that are meaningful, confidence-building, and rooted in how real learning happens.

Selected Experience

  • Lead Teacher & Program Developer — Visionary Village Santa Cruz (2020–2023)
  • Program Coordinator & Lead Instructor — Family Food Security & Nutrition Series, Siskiyou Food Assistance (2025–Present)
  • Educational Consultant & Tutor — Independent practice, including Scripture Explorers literacy program
  • Ecology Teacher & Physical Education Specialist — Mulberry School / Discovery Charter (2005–2008)
  • Health & Wellness Coach / Educator — Gut Healing Wisdom (ongoing)
