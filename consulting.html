Educational Consulting & Tutoring

Personalized learning support rooted in real classroom experience, curriculum design, and a deep respect for how children learn best.

Tutoring & Academic Support

I offer one-on-one and small-group tutoring tailored to each student’s needs, strengths, and learning style. Areas of focus include:

  • Reading fluency, comprehension, and literacy development
  • Constructivist math for students who struggle with traditional approaches
  • Critical thinking, speech, and debate
  • American History and Constitution studies
  • Faith-integrated and nature-based literacy programs (including Scripture Explorers)
  • Support for students with IEPs and individualized learning needs

Sessions can take place in-person in the Mount Shasta area or remotely. Learning is designed to be engaging, confidence-building, and connected to real life.

Curriculum Design & Program Development

With experience as Lead Teacher and Program Developer at Visionary Village Santa Cruz and in non-profit education settings, I design complete learning experiences that combine academic rigor with creativity and joy.

Examples of past work include:

  • A middle-school critical thinking and debate program that taught propaganda detection and logical fallacies through outdoor games and creative projects
  • An immersive American History & Constitution course in which students researched and portrayed Revolutionary-era figures in costume
  • Hands-on cooking, nutrition, and permaculture courses that integrated math, reading, and practical life skills
  • Nature-based exploration classes focused on local wildlife and medicinal plants
  • Grant-funded community education series for families

Educational Consulting for Families & Organizations

I work with homeschooling families, parents navigating IEPs, and non-profits that want to create meaningful educational programs. Whether you need help designing a custom learning path, choosing approaches that fit your child, or building a community education series, I bring both formal training (M.A. Education, UC Santa Cruz) and years of practical experience.
