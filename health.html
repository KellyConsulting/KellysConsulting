Health Coaching

Personalized support for adults and families seeking better health through practical nutrition and sustainable habits.

I offer personalized health and wellness coaching for adults and families, with a special focus on practical nutrition and sustainable lifestyle change.

My approach is grounded in real-world experience. After helping my husband recover from two serious illnesses that conventional medicine had considered incurable, I dedicated myself to understanding how food, fermentation, and daily habits can support healing. That journey continues to shape the way I work with clients today.

Who I Work With

  • Adults seeking better energy, digestion, and overall wellness
  • Families wanting to implement healing or elimination diets
  • Parents navigating food allergies, sensitivities, and dietary restrictions with their children

What Coaching Looks Like

Sessions are practical, compassionate, and tailored to your real life. We focus on clear action steps, meal planning, kitchen confidence, and building habits that last — whether the goal is personal healing or supporting a child’s specialized dietary needs.

This work naturally complements my educational consulting and tutoring practice, especially when families are looking for both academic support and guidance around food and health.
